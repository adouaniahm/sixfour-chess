Privacy Policy for SixFour Chess

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Publisher: ADOUANI SAS

App: SixFour Chess

Overview

SixFour Chess is designed to be local-first. By default, game data stays on your device. We do not use advertising SDKs or social media SDKs.

No advertising identifiers

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data for offline play.

Optional features

  • Local game history and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Master difficulty may send only the current chess position in FEN format to external analysis services.
  • Consent-driven analytics and crash reporting may be enabled only if you accept them in the app.

Third-party services

  • Lichess Cloud Eval: https://lichess.org/api/cloud-eval
  • Stockfish Online: https://stockfish.online/api/s/v2.php

Local storage

Game history, settings, and snapshots remain on the device unless you uninstall the app or delete them from inside the app.
